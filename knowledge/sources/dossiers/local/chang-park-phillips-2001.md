# Chang, Park, and Phillips (2001)

## Bibliographic identity

Chang, Y., Park, J. Y., and Phillips, P. C. B. (2001), “Nonlinear
Econometric Models with Cointegrated and Deterministically Trending
Regressors,” *The Econometrics Journal* 4, 1–36.

## Main contributions

- Develops asymptotics for nonlinear regressions with multiple \(I(1)\)
  regressors.
- Makes convergence rates depend on the degree of asymptotic homogeneity.
- Shows why ordinary nonlinear least squares generally does not deliver valid
  conventional inference under endogenous stochastic trends.

## Critical insights for the I(2) validation

A cross-product is a degree-two homogeneous map of a vector of integrated
levels. Its \(O_p(T)\) magnitude follows from nonlinear-transform limit theory;
that magnitude is not a conventional unit-root classification.

## Formal results, assumptions, and rank conditions

The relevant foundation is a joint invariance principle for the integrated
regressors plus regularity and nondegeneracy of the homogeneous transformation.
The paper does not by itself establish the exact four-column full-design
condition used by this project.

## Limitations and prohibited inferences

Do not read the paper as a cross-product-specific IM-OLS, residual-test, or
state-dependent inference theorem.
