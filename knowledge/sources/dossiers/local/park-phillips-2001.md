# Park and Phillips (2001)

## Bibliographic identity

Park, J. Y., and Phillips, P. C. B. (2001), “Nonlinear Regressions with
Integrated Time Series,” *Econometrica* 69, 117–161.

## Main contributions

- Provides limit theory for nonlinear functions of integrated regressors.
- Separates integrable transformations from asymptotically homogeneous ones.
- Derives nonstandard rates directly from the transformation’s homogeneity.

## Critical insights for the I(2) validation

The interaction rate must be derived as a nonlinear transformation of the joint
integrated vector. It is not licensed by treating the product as another
ordinary \(I(1)\) innovation or as a classical \(I(2)\) level.

## Formal results, assumptions, and rank conditions

The theory requires an invariance principle, regular transformations, and
nondegenerate limiting functionals. Rank of the project’s complete normalized
design remains a separate condition.

## Limitations and prohibited inferences

This source does not validate formal FM-OLS or IM-OLS formulas for the exact
lagged cross-product.
