# Stypka and Wagner (2019)

## Bibliographic identity

Stypka, O., and Wagner, M. (2019), “The Phillips Unit Root Tests for
Polynomials of Integrated Processes Revisited,” *Economics Letters* 176,
109–113.

## Main contributions

- Derives kernel-weighted limits for powers of integrated processes.
- Shows that polynomial transformations require their own unit-root-test
  asymptotics.
- Extends the relevant results to linear-process innovations.

## Critical insights for the I(2) validation

Polynomial magnitude does not turn a nonlinear transform into a conventional
integrated process. Unit-root procedures must be derived for the transformation
rather than assigned from its \(T\)-order.

## Formal results, assumptions, and rank conditions

The published results concern powers and polynomial transformations. They
support the classification warning but not the exact cross-product estimator.

## Limitations and prohibited inferences

Do not use this paper to claim cross-product-specific CPR critical values or an
IM-OLS residual adjustment.
